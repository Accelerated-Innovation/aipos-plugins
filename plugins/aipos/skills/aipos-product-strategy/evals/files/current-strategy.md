# Product Opportunity Brief — Hand-off Hub

Canonical reference: strategy/handoff-hub/product-opportunity-brief.md
Revision: 3, 2026-09-01
Content status: agreed hypothesis. Strategy owner: PM Dana.

Vision: make supplier handoffs dependable for small agencies.
Adopted initial segment: independent design agencies with 5–20 staff.
Outside initial focus: large agencies and solo designers.
Need hypothesis [A]: small agencies lose client trust because supplier handoffs
are difficult to track. Existing workflow: email and a shared spreadsheet,
reported in INT-1 and INT-2 (interview reports, not measured behavior).
Concept [A]: a shared handoff workspace makes missed handoffs visible early.
Differentiation [A]: worthwhile improvement over maintaining the spreadsheet.
Commercial model [A]: agency subscription; price and acquisition cost unknown.
Customer outcome: fewer missed handoffs. Business outcome: sustainable paid use.
Baseline missed-handoff rate: unknown. No numeric success threshold adopted.
First risk: whether agencies will replace their current workaround.

Learning authorization L-3 from Dana: one two-week pilot with six agencies,
ending 2026-09-20. The supplied authorization is only for this pilot.
Initiative canvas: solution-framing/handoff-visibility/canvas.json (reference only).
No approval of production scope has been supplied. No system writes requested.
